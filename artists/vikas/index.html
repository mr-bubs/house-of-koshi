VIKAS

Quiet presence.
Genuine warmth.

Actor Represented by Koshi Artists
Artist

Nothing performed.
Nothing absent.

Vikas is a 30-year-old actor with a quiet, grounded presence and an instinct for restrained, attentive performance. Naturally observant and understated, he brings warmth to the screen without demanding its attention, allowing smaller shifts in expression, timing and body language to carry the moment. His work can move easily between dry humour, curiosity, affection and more serious emotional territory while retaining an unforced, distinctly human quality.
