About / AI & Virtual Talent

A fictional studio.
A real creative project.

House of Koshi is a fictional entertainment and talent studio developed as an AI-assisted creative project. Its artists, productions and studio world are fictional, while the creative work used to build that world is real.

Virtual talent

Characters treated as
creative performers.

The people represented by Koshi Artists are virtual, fictional performers developed through writing, visual creation and AI-assisted creative tools.

Within the House of Koshi website they are presented through the language of artist representation, productions, portfolios and editorials. That presentation is part of the fictional studio concept rather than a claim that the artists are real human performers.

Creative framework

HOW TO READ THE PROJECT

01

The studio is fictional.

House of Koshi, its divisions and its represented virtual artists form a fictional entertainment-studio universe.

02

The creative project is real.

The writing, visual development, design, adaptation, world-building and website are genuine creative work developed for the project.

03

AI is part of the process.

AI-assisted tools are used as part of the project's creative workflow, including visual development and storytelling.

04

Adaptations remain attributed.

When House of Koshi presents a creative adaptation of another author's work, the source work and author remain identified and credited.

Presentation

The fiction works better
when it is allowed to be fiction.

Most House of Koshi pages therefore present the studio world directly. Artist pages read like artist portfolios. Studio pages read like production pages. Editorial work can treat the artists as performers within that creative universe.

This page exists so that the nature of the project remains clear without requiring every normal page to interrupt the fictional presentation with repeated AI disclaimers.

Creative adaptations

Source work stays visible.

House of Koshi distinguishes between original productions and creative adaptations. Adaptation pages and the House of Koshi reader retain source attribution where applicable, including links to the credited source when available.
