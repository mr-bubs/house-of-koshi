The house

Artists.
Stories. Worlds.

House of Koshi brings artist representation, original productions, creative adaptations and contemporary visual media under one creative house.

Its artists can move between performance, production, editorial work and other forms of creative expression without being defined by a single role or story.

Philosophy

Talent came first.
Everything else followed.

House of Koshi begins with the artist. Productions, editorial work and larger creative worlds grow outward from the talent rather than reducing the talent to a single project.

Heritage mark

Shawna / The original mark.

The original hand-drawn monochrome portrait of Shawna remains part of House of Koshi as a heritage mark. It is not the primary logo or mascot; the House of Koshi wordmark remains the central identity of the studio.
